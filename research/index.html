Research

Research focus

Research themes in speech, language, medical imaging, and multimodal biomarkers for clinically meaningful machine learning, including recent work on large language models.

Paula A. Pérez-Toro studies how speech, language, and multimodal signals can support earlier and more interpretable assessment of neurological and mental health conditions. Her work combines pattern recognition, clinical machine learning, cross-lingual modeling, large language model adaptation, and medical imaging analysis for translational healthcare applications.

Neurodegenerative disease assessment

Speech and language markers for Alzheimer disease, Parkinson disease, and related disorders.

Multilingual and cross-lingual modeling

Generalizable modeling across languages with attention to bias and realistic deployment.

Interpretable clinical machine learning

Feature sets and models designed to support clinical understanding, not only accuracy.

LLMs for speech and language health applications

Research on instruction-tuned and large language models for multilingual and disordered speech analysis.

Medical imaging and vocal tract MRI

Research linking speech production, vocal tract MRI, generative imaging, and data-driven support for therapy and diagnosis.
